How it works

Every problem follows the same accountable path, from first report to real-world implementation.

01

Identify a problem

Anyone can report a societal challenge they've witnessed or experienced.

02

Submit evidence

Supporting documents and context help others understand the scale and urgency.

03

AI categorizes the challenge

The problem is routed to a sector, and similar existing problems are surfaced.

04

Community prioritizes it

Votes, comments and evidence shape a priority score set by genuine engagement.

05

Teams build solutions

Students, researchers, industry professionals and universities form teams to respond.

06

Experts evaluate

Authorized NGOs, government bodies and industry experts compare submissions.

07

Best solution gets implemented

The selected solution moves into tracked, real-world implementation.

Who can participate

The platform is built for anyone who can either surface a problem or help solve one.

Citizens

Report the problems affecting your community and back them with evidence.

Students

Apply coursework and emerging skills to problems that matter, and build a track record.

Researchers

Bring domain expertise and rigor to challenges that need deeper investigation.

Universities

Mobilize departments and labs toward problems aligned with your institution's expertise.

Industry

Contribute technical resources, mentorship and scale to promising solutions.

NGOs / Government

Evaluate submissions with domain authority and carry the winning solution into practice.
